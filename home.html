Why Visit Kerala?

stunning natural beauty, especially the serene backwaters, lush tea plantations (Munnar), and rich wildlife (Thekkady, Wayanad), alongside unique cultural experiences like Ayurveda, Kathakali dance, spice markets, and historic Kochi, offering a blend of relaxation, adventure, and cultural immersion in "God's Own Country"

Best Time to Visit!

The best time to visit Kerala is October to March, when the weather is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing, beaches, and backwaters. The monsoon season (June to September) brings lush greenery and is perfect for Ayurveda lovers. Summer (April to May) is hot and humid, best for hill stations like Munnar and Wayanad

Culture

Kerala’s vibrant culture shines through festivals like Onam and Vishu, with flower carpets, boat races, and traditional feasts. It’s also famous for Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, Theyyam, and colorful temple celebrations.
